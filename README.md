Smart Ansu Koroma

Sierra Leonean Student Leader | Public Speaker | Founder | Footballer

Smart Ansu Koroma is a young Sierra Leonean student leader, public speaker, entrepreneur, and footballer with a strong interest in education, leadership, public speaking, youth development, and community activities.

He is the CEO and Founder of Smart Kay Public Speaking Organisation, a public speaking and learning platform focused on developing confidence, communication skills, leadership, and intellectual growth among young people.

Smart has also been involved in student leadership and school debate activities at Lungi International High School for Science and Technology, where he has participated in initiatives aimed at encouraging discipline, education, leadership, and public speaking.

He is also the Founder of Legacy Xpert Guys, a youth-oriented initiative focused on bringing young people together around personal development, learning, and positive community engagement.

Beyond academics and leadership, Smart has a passion for football and has played competitive football, including experience as a youth team captain.

His personal motto is:

“This world ya anything na time.”

Smart Ansu Koroma continues to focus on education, leadership, public speaking, youth development, football, and building opportunities for the next generation in Sierra Leone.
